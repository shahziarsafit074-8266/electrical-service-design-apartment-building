# Electrical Service Design of a Residential Apartment Building with 11 kV Substation and Lightning Protection System

## Project Overview

This project presents a complete electrical service design for a modern residential apartment building. The design covers electrical power distribution, lighting and fixture systems, communication infrastructure, lightning protection and grounding, protection systems, and an 11/0.415 kV substation.

The project combines engineering calculations with detailed electrical drawings to develop a safe, reliable, and practical electrical service system for a residential building.

## Project Objectives

- Develop complete electrical service layouts for a residential apartment building.
- Design lighting, power, and communication circuits.
- Calculate connected and design loads.
- Determine suitable wire and cable sizes.
- Perform voltage-drop analysis.
- Select appropriate MCBs and protection equipment.
- Design lightning protection and grounding systems.
- Develop an 11/0.415 kV substation and Single Line Diagram (SLD).
- Prepare a detailed Bill of Materials (BOM) and cost estimation.
- Consider BNBC 2020 requirements and electrical safety.

## Main Design Components

### Electrical Conduit & Fixture Layout

The electrical layouts include lighting points, ceiling fans, wall lights, tube lights, 2-pin and 3-pin sockets, AC connections, oven, refrigerator, washing machine, TV, CCTV, switchboards, distribution boards, and electrical conduit routing.

Separate layouts are provided for apartment floors, ground-floor areas, and the top-floor/roof lighting system.

### Communication Layout

The project includes structured communication arrangements for telephone, internet, intercom, television, and communication panels. Power and communication circuits are arranged separately to reduce interference.

### Lightning Protection & Earthing

The lightning protection and grounding design includes lightning arresters, electrode arrangements, earthing conductors, earth electrodes, and grounding connections.

### Load Calculation

Detailed lighting and power load calculations are performed for the residential floors and common areas.

- Typical apartment lighting load: **10,110 W**
- Typical apartment power load: **67,740 W**
- Ground-floor connected load: **1,580 W**
- Top-floor roof lighting load: **540 W**

## Wire & Cable Sizing

Wire and cable sizes are selected using design current, current-carrying capacity, and voltage-drop considerations.

The design includes sizing for lighting, fan, socket, AC, oven, refrigerator, washing machine, and other power circuits.

Example final sizes include:

- Lighting/Fan/CCTV: **1.5 mm²**
- Socket circuits: **1.5 mm²**
- AC circuits: **4.0 mm²**
- Power sockets and major appliances: **2.5 mm²**

## Protection & Distribution

The protection system includes:

- **6 A SP C-Curve MCB** for lighting circuits
- **16 A SP C-Curve MCB** for socket circuits
- **20 A SP C-Curve MCB** for oven/power circuits
- **25 A DP C-Curve MCB** for AC circuits
- **40 A, 30 mA RCCB**
- Distribution boards for apartment electrical distribution

## 11/0.415 kV Substation Design

A dedicated 11/0.415 kV substation is designed as part of the project.

### Load & Transformer Selection

- Connected load: **1000 kW**
- Power factor: **0.85**
- Apparent load: **1176.5 kVA**
- Future allowance: **15%**
- Maximum demand: **1353 kVA**
- Selected transformer: **2000 kVA**

### Transformer & Major Equipment

**Power Transformer:** 2000 kVA, 11/0.415 kV

Major equipment includes:

- 11 kV Vacuum Circuit Breaker (VCB)
- 11 kV isolators
- Lightning arresters
- Current Transformers (CT)
- Potential Transformer (PT)
- 415 V LT panel / MDB
- Air Circuit Breaker (ACB)
- Aluminium busbar system
- HT and LT XLPE cables
- Earthing system

Selected ratings include:

- HT VCB: **11 kV, 630 A, 25 kA**
- LT ACB: **415 V, 3200 A, 50 kA**
- HT CT: **150/5 A**
- LT CT: **3500/5 A**

## Single Line Diagram (SLD)

The 11 kV substation SLD shows the major power-system components and their interconnections, including incoming supply, HT protection, isolators, lightning arresters, CT/PT, 11 kV bus, transformer, LT bus, main LT protection, and outgoing feeders.

## Common Areas

Separate electrical service design is provided for the parking area, guard room, guest room, toilet, stair area, and common areas.

## Bill of Materials (BOM) & Cost Estimation

A detailed BOM is prepared for lighting and socket circuits, power-load circuits, wires, conduits, MCBs, RCCB, distribution boards, earthing equipment, transformer, VCB, isolators, lightning arresters, CT/PT, HT/LT cables, LT panel, ACB, and busbar systems.

The BOM includes quantities, unit rates, and estimated costs.

## Design Standards & Safety

The design considers:

- BNBC 2020 requirements
- Electrical installation safety
- Proper earthing and grounding
- Voltage-drop limitations
- Suitable conductor sizing
- Overcurrent and fault protection
- Separation of power and communication circuits
- Safe and reliable electrical distribution
